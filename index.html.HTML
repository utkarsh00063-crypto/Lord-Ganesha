🙏 About Ganesh Chaturthi

Ganesh Chaturthi is one of the most beautiful festivals celebrated in India. It is dedicated to Lord Ganesha, the God of wisdom, knowledge and prosperity. Devotees bring Lord Ganesha idols to their homes and community pandals and perform prayers, aarti and cultural programs.
